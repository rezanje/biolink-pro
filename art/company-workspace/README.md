# Synthetic company workspace screenshots

`desktop-dashboard.png`, `mobile-dashboard.png`, `desktop-leads.png`, and `mobile-leads.png` show a local review fixture. The company uses the source-backed CNY name and original logo; every person, lead, status, count, and date in this fixture is explicitly fabricated for review. No production company, profile, or lead record was read or changed. The browser mocked the company workspace API, then checked page overflow and keyboard focus at 1360px and 390px widths. Screenshots were captured from the local development server.
